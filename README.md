One sentence, at most 200 characters, that makes a small everyday moment
strange and new. This entry takes the most ordinary machine in daily life —
the office lift — and gives it one unexplained stop: a floor nobody pressed,
doors opening onto nothing, and a whole building briefly holding its breath.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) writes the sentence to `entry.txt` in
the output directory, clearing the directory's contents first and adding no
trailing newline.
